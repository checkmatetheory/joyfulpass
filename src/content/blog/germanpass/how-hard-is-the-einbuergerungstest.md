I tell every group the same thing: this is one of the fairest exams you'll ever sit. That doesn't mean you can skip preparation — but it does mean preparation works.

## Why it's manageable

- **The questions are public.** Every question comes from the official BAMF catalogue, so nothing on the day should be new.
- **The pass mark is forgiving.** You need **17 out of 33** — just over half.
- **There's time.** Sixty minutes for 33 multiple-choice questions is generous.

For the full format, see [how many questions are on the Einbürgerungstest](/germanpass/einbuergerungstest/blog/how-many-questions-einbuergerungstest/).

## What still catches people out

- **Negative questions.** Wording like "which is *not*…" or "what is *forbidden*…" flips the right answer. Read those twice.
- **The state questions.** Three questions are about your federal state. They're easy marks if you practise them — and easy to forget.
- **Politics and history.** The Basic Law, the Bundestag and Bundesrat, and German history carry precise facts worth learning properly.
- **Old or invented question lists.** Practise only material tied to the current catalogue.

## How to know you're ready

Aim for 17 to be your floor, not your target, across several full practice sets. Use the [free Einbürgerungstest practice tests](/germanpass/einbuergerungstest/practice/), the [sample questions and answers](/germanpass/einbuergerungstest/questions/) and our [study guide](/germanpass/einbuergerungstest/blog/einbuergerungstest-study-guide/).

Confirm current details with the [BAMF](https://www.bamf.de/EN/Themen/Integration/ZugewanderteTeilnehmende/Einbuergerung/einbuergerung-node.html). GermanPass is an independent study app.
