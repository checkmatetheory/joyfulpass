If you're preparing for German naturalisation, the first thing to nail down is the format of the Einbürgerungstest: how many questions, and how many you need to get right.

## The short answer

- **33 questions** in total
- **17 correct** to pass
- **60 minutes** to complete it
- Multiple choice, drawn from the official BAMF question catalogue

Of the 33 questions, **30 are general** (the same across Germany) and **3 are specific to your federal state** (Bundesland).

## Where the questions come from

Every question is taken from a public catalogue of **300 general questions**, plus **10 questions for each of the 16 federal states**. Because the catalogue is public, there's nothing on the test you can't see and practise in advance — which is exactly why regular practice works so well.

## What the questions cover

The general questions fall into a few themes:

- **Politics and democracy** — how Germany is governed, elections, and institutions
- **History and responsibility** — the Nazi era, division, and reunification
- **People and society** — rights, freedoms, and the Basic Law (Grundgesetz)

The 3 state questions cover the Bundesland where you take the test.

## How to make sure you get 17+

The pass mark leaves room for mistakes, but the strongest candidates don't lean on that. Aim to score comfortably above 17 on full mock tests before you book — that buffer absorbs any test-day nerves.
