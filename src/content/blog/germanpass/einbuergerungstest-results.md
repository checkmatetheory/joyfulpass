One thing surprises almost everyone in my groups: you walk out of the Einbürgerungstest **without** knowing your score.

## How long the result takes

Your answer sheet is evaluated centrally by the BAMF, not at the test centre. The result and — if you passed — your certificate then arrive **by post**, typically a few weeks later. Many people report around four to eight weeks, depending on the centre and time of year.

## What you receive

- If you pass (17 or more out of 33), you get a **certificate** confirming it. Keep it safe — you'll need it for your naturalisation application.
- If you don't pass, you're told the result by post too, so you know where you stand before rebooking.

The certificate **doesn't expire**, so it stays valid however long your application takes.

## If you didn't pass

You can retake the test — there's no limit on attempts, though each one is a new booking and fee. Use your result to see how far off you were, then read [what happens if you fail the Einbürgerungstest](/germanpass/einbuergerungstest/blog/einbuergerungstest-what-if-you-fail/) for a plan.

## Next step: your application

With your certificate, you can complete your naturalisation application with your local Einbürgerungsbehörde. Check the current requirements with the [BAMF](https://www.bamf.de/EN/Themen/Integration/ZugewanderteTeilnehmende/Einbuergerung/einbuergerung-node.html).
