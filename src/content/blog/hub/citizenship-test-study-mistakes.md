Preparing for a citizenship or settlement test is easier when you know which habits waste time. These seven mistakes appear often in study plans that leave people feeling busy but not ready.

## 1. Starting with random online quizzes

Quizzes can be useful, but not if you do not know whether their answers are current or connected to the official source. Read the official guide first, then use practice questions to test your recall.

## 2. Studying only the easy topics

It is natural to return to material you already know. Instead, track incorrect answers and spend extra time on your weakest chapters.

## 3. Memorising letter choices

Question order and wording can change. Learn why an answer is right, not just whether it was option A, B, C, or D.

## 4. Skipping timed mock tests

Knowing facts at home is different from recalling them within an exam time limit. Timed practice gives you a realistic view of your readiness.

## 5. Waiting until the booking is close

Short, regular study sessions are easier to retain than a rushed final week. Give yourself enough time to revisit difficult topics.

## 6. Treating a single pass as proof you are ready

One good practice score can be luck. Aim for a consistent margin above the official pass mark before you book or attend.

## 7. Ignoring official updates

Rules, fees, and test-day procedures can change. Check official government guidance whenever you are making a booking or submitting an application.
