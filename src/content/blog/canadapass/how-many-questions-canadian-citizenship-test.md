If you've received your invitation to take the Canadian citizenship test, the first thing to nail down is the format: how many questions, and how many you need to get right.

## The short answer

- **20 questions** in total
- **15 correct** to pass (that's **75%**)
- **30 minutes** to complete it
- Multiple choice and true/false, based on the official **Discover Canada** guide

Every question comes straight from *Discover Canada: The Rights and Responsibilities of Citizenship* — the free study guide published by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). There's nothing on the test that isn't in that guide.

## What the questions cover

The 20 questions are drawn from across the whole guide, including:

- The rights and responsibilities of citizenship
- Canada's history and the people who shaped it
- How Canadians govern themselves — federal, provincial, and municipal
- Canadian symbols, geography, and regions
- Voting and how elections work

You won't know which topics come up, so being comfortable across the entire guide beats memorising a few chapters and hoping.

## How the test is given

Most applicants take the test online or in person, depending on how IRCC schedules it. Either way it's the same structure: 20 questions, 30 minutes, 15 to pass. If English or French isn't your first language, remember the questions are written in plain language — but the wording still rewards careful reading.

## How to make sure you get 15+

The pass mark leaves room for five mistakes, but the strongest candidates don't lean on that. People who pass first time are usually scoring **17 or 18 out of 20 on mock tests** before their appointment, which absorbs the odd nervous slip on the day.

[CanadaPass](/canadian-citizenship-test/) mock tests mirror the real 20-question, 30-minute, 75%-to-pass format, so by the time you sit the official test the structure already feels familiar.

## Before your appointment

Study from the official source and confirm current details here: [Discover Canada / citizenship test — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/become-canadian-citizen/citizenship-test.html). Then work through full mock tests until 15 out of 20 feels comfortable — and aim higher.
