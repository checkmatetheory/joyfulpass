An unsuccessful citizenship test result is disappointing, but it does not automatically end your application. IRCC normally gives eligible applicants another opportunity to show they meet the knowledge requirement. The exact next step depends on your file and the instructions you receive.

## What happens after an unsuccessful test?

IRCC will tell you what happens next through your account, email, or other official communication. In many cases, applicants who do not pass their first written test are invited to take it again. If an applicant is unsuccessful again, IRCC may arrange an interview with a citizenship official instead of another written test.

Do not rely on a general timeline from a friend or social media post. Processing times and arrangements can differ. Your notice from IRCC is the source of truth.

## Can you take the Canadian citizenship test again?

Usually, yes. The process and timing for a second attempt are set by IRCC. Read every message carefully and keep your contact details current so you do not miss a deadline.

Review the official [citizenship test information from IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/become-canadian-citizen/citizenship-test.html) for current rules. For what to expect during an appointment, see our guide to [Canadian citizenship test day](/canadapass/canadian-citizenship-test/blog/citizenship-test-day-tips/).

## How to prepare differently for a retest

Simply repeating the same random quizzes may not solve the issue. Instead, use your first attempt as a signal to improve your method.

- **Return to Discover Canada.** Read the official guide, not just summaries.
- **Sort weak topics.** Track whether history, government, geography, or citizenship responsibilities cause the most errors.
- **Practise under test conditions.** Work through 20 questions in 30 minutes without notes.
- **Review explanations.** Understanding why an answer is correct matters more than memorising a letter choice.
- **Use spaced revision.** Revisit difficult facts over several days instead of trying to learn everything in one evening.

The pass mark is 15 out of 20. Aim to score higher than that consistently in practice, so a few unfamiliar questions on the day do not derail you. Our overview of the [Canadian citizenship test pass mark and format](/canadapass/canadian-citizenship-test/blog/canadian-citizenship-test-pass-mark/) can help you set that target.

## Stay focused on the official process

Passing the knowledge test is only one part of a citizenship application. IRCC may still need to confirm other eligibility requirements. Keep copies of official correspondence and respond promptly if it asks for information.
