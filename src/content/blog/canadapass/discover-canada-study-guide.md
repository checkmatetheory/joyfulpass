Discover Canada is the official study guide for the Canadian citizenship test, and it's long. You don't need to memorize it page by page — you need to know which chapters carry the most test weight.

## Where questions concentrate

- **Rights and responsibilities of citizenship** — consistently tested, including the responsibilities specific to voting and jury duty
- **How Canadians govern themselves** — federal, provincial, and municipal structure, plus the role of the monarchy
- **Canadian history** — Confederation, the World Wars, and the development of the ten provinces and three territories
- **Who we are** — Indigenous peoples, French and English heritage, and Canada's diversity

## A study approach that works

1. Read one chapter of Discover Canada at a time.
2. Immediately take a [CanadaPass](/canadian-citizenship-test/) practice set covering just that chapter.
3. Note which questions you missed and reread only that section.
4. Move to the next chapter once you're above 90% on the current one.

This mirrors how the official test is actually written — questions map closely to specific Discover Canada sections rather than general knowledge of Canada.
