Waiting for a result is often harder than the test itself. Here's what to expect.

## How you get your result

IRCC communicates your result through official channels — usually by updating your application in your online account and/or by letter. Timings vary, so don't rely on what a friend experienced; follow what IRCC tells you. IRCC's [citizenship test page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/become-canadian-citizen/citizenship-test.html) has the current process.

You need at least **15 out of 20** to pass.

## If you pass

Passing the test is one requirement, not the whole decision. If you meet all the other requirements, the next step is usually an invitation to a **citizenship ceremony**, where you take the Oath of Citizenship and become a Canadian citizen.

## If you don't pass

It's usually not the end of your application — IRCC normally gives eligible applicants another opportunity. Our guide on [what happens if you fail the citizenship test](/canadapass/canadian-citizenship-test/blog/what-happens-if-you-fail-citizenship-test/) explains the usual next steps and how to prepare differently.

## Getting ready for a second attempt

Go back to Discover Canada, focus on your weakest topics, and practise until you're comfortably above 15 out of 20 on several different sets. Try the [free practice tests](/canadapass/canadian-citizenship-test/practice/) and the [sample questions and answers](/canadapass/canadian-citizenship-test/questions/).
