The applicants I saw who struggled rarely found the questions conceptually difficult. They struggled because they'd studied one part of Discover Canada well and skipped the rest.

## What makes it challenging

- **Breadth.** Questions can come from any part of the official guide — history, how Parliament works, the provinces and territories, rights and responsibilities, symbols.
- **A demanding pass mark.** You need 15 out of 20 (75%). Five wrong answers is the limit.
- **Specific facts.** Dates, names and "which of these" questions reward precise knowledge.

## What makes it manageable

- **One official source.** The test is based on the *Discover Canada* study guide, so there's a defined amount to learn.
- **A known format.** Twenty questions in 30 minutes. See the [pass mark and format guide](/canadapass/canadian-citizenship-test/blog/canadian-citizenship-test-pass-mark/).
- **Recall improves quickly with practice.**

## How to know you're ready

Aim to clear 15 out of 20 comfortably across several different practice sets, not just once. Use the [free Canadian citizenship practice tests](/canadapass/canadian-citizenship-test/practice/), the [sample questions and answers](/canadapass/canadian-citizenship-test/questions/), and a structured [Discover Canada study plan](/canadapass/canadian-citizenship-test/blog/discover-canada-study-plan/).
